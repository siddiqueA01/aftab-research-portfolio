THE FOUNDATION / PUBLISHED RESEARCH

Ambition needs evidence.

The next research direction starts with understanding what the current results can—and cannot—tell us.

01 / BIOELECTRICAL IMPEDANCE

Reading tissue.
Testing the limits.

Machine learning and multi-frequency impedance for caseous lymphadenitis screening in goats.

Evidence & context

36 goats; 2,673 measurements. Five-fold ROC-AUC: 0.851. Animal-held-out LOSO ROC-AUC: 0.68. Research-stage screening evidence requiring external validation.

02 / SMARTPHONE IMAGING

Blood spots.
Biological clues.

Exploring anemia signatures through prepared blood-spot images in goats, sheep, and cattle.

Evidence & context

Image-level performance varies across species. Prospective field validation is needed before diagnostic deployment.

Read the publication ↗
03 / BEHAVIORAL ANALYTICS

Everyday activity.
New questions.

Time-aware learning for behavioral anomaly monitoring and short-horizon forecasting in goats.

Evidence & context

30 Boer goats; 85,890 RFID records. Five-minute onset ROC-AUC: 0.869; ten-minute: 0.841. Behavioral forecasting does not establish disease diagnosis.

03 / THE BOLD INNOVATOR

From promising signals
to better questions.
Then better tools.

The next step is to connect measurements, models, and field realities—without losing sight of what still needs to be tested.

01 / CONNECT THE SIGNALS

Multimodal thinking.

Explore how complementary sensing modalities can describe animal health more fully than one measurement alone.

02 / CHALLENGE THE MODEL

Validation that matters.

Prioritize held-out animals, new settings, interpretable models, and clear limits on what results establish.

03 / MOVE TOWARD USE

Practical questions first.

Work toward accessible sensing and decision support that respects biological expertise and real operating conditions.

These are research priorities and future directions, not claims of validated commercial products.
