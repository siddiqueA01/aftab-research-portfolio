01 / SELECTED DISCIPLINES

Science doesn't
stay in its lane.

The work spans seemingly separate fields, but each project follows the same principle: define the biological question, evaluate the signal, and challenge the model's limits.

RESEARCH DOMAIN / 01

Signals hidden in animal physiology.

Bioelectrical impedance, radiofrequency sensing, blood-pattern imaging, and interpretable models for precision livestock research.

ANIMAL HEALTH · AI

Start with animal-level validation and reference physiology. Compare sensor signals with established measures; don't mistake a promising classifier for a clinical diagnostic.

RESEARCH DOMAIN / 02

Visual intelligence for safer food.

Spectral and image-based inspection, machine learning, and quantitative methods applied to food systems and biological materials.

FOOD SCIENCE · VISION

Evaluate imaging systems under realistic variation, including independent samples, production settings, and interpretable error analysis.

RESEARCH DOMAIN / 03

Intelligence inside ecological systems.

Entomology, arthropod pest management, biological control, and data-supported decision-making for agricultural resilience.

ENTOMOLOGY · IPM

Link treatment performance to host plants, field context, and uncertainty. Use transparent models to support—but not replace—biological expertise.
