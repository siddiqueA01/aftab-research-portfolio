GOATS / BIOELECTRICAL IMPEDANCE / MACHINE LEARNING

A different way
to read tissue.

Caseous lymphadenitis screening is a biological challenge. This study explored whether multi-frequency impedance measurements could provide a useful signal for machine-learning classification.

Study scale
36 goats 2,673 measurements
Five-fold CV
0.851 ROC-AUC
Animal-held-out
0.68 LOSO ROC-AUC

The important finding: performance dropped when testing on held-out animals. That gap matters when considering use beyond the study herd.

SMARTPHONE IMAGING / GOATS · SHEEP · CATTLE

A drop of blood.
A different perspective.

Could the visual patterns in prepared blood spots carry information about anemia? This work pairs smartphone imaging with machine learning to investigate that possibility across three livestock species.

What the comparison teaches: performance varies across species. A strong image-level result in one species does not establish field performance in another.
